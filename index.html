Three languages, one very proud kid

Every lesson is hands on. No slides, no memorizing, just building.

H

HTML: the bones

Kids learn to structure a page the way you'd frame a treehouse, headings, paragraphs, images and buttons all snapping into place.

C

CSS: the personality

This is where the page gets color, spacing and style. It's usually the moment a "boring" page turns into something a kid wants to show off.

JS

JavaScript: the spark

Buttons that respond, quizzes that check answers, games that react. This is where kids realize they can make a machine listen to them.

How a beginner gets from zero to "I built this"
